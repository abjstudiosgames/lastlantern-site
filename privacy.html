Last Lantern privacy policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026

Who we are

ABJ Studios Games operates Last Lantern and is responsible for the personal information described here. Contact us at abjstudiosgames@gmail.com.

Read this policy at https://abjstudiosgames.github.io/lastlantern-site/privacy.html. Account deletion instructions are at https://abjstudiosgames.github.io/lastlantern-site/delete-account.html.

Account and game information

Last Lantern requires an internet connection. Unity Gaming Services (UGS) creates an anonymous account with a player ID and authentication session. You do not give us an email address to sign in. Your player ID still links your activity to your account. The app stores sign-in information and game caches on your device.

Unity Cloud Code processes game actions, and Unity Cloud Save stores your city, resources, buildings, citizens, troops, heroes, equipment, formations, tutorial and battle progress. We also process your player name, alliance membership, roles, help actions and alliance chat, leaderboard scores, world-map city position, research, marches and rally activity. We use timestamps, command receipts and security counters to save progress, prevent duplicate rewards and investigate abuse.

Other players can see your player name, leaderboard entries and world-map city position. Alliance members can see the alliance roster and messages you send to alliance chat; alliance names and tags appear in public search. A world-map position is a game coordinate, not your physical location. Avoid putting private information in your player name or chat.

Unity receives connection and technical information, including IP addresses, service identifiers and operational logs, to run and protect the online services. We do not access your device location, GPS or contacts. With analytics consent, Unity Analytics can derive an approximate region from your IP address, as explained below. We do not show ads or collect data for advertising profiles.

Optional analytics and feedback

We use UGS Analytics only after you agree on first launch. You can decline and still play, or change your choice in the in-game Settings analytics control at any time. Turning analytics off stops further analytics collection; it does not turn off the account and game processing needed for online play. You can ask us to delete analytics already collected using the contact above or delete your account.

Our gameplay analytics events are first_open, tutorial_step, tutorial_complete, session_start, building_upgrade, battle_result, march_started and alliance_join. They describe first use, tutorial progress, sessions, upgrades, battle outcomes, marches and alliance joins so we can find problems and improve the game. Unity also processes SDK session events, player and installation identifiers, device and app details, diagnostics, performance information and IP-derived approximate region when analytics is enabled. We do not send chat text, feedback emails or your player name as custom analytics event content.

The Feedback button opens an email you can review and send to abjstudiosgames@gmail.com. It includes game version, device model, Lantern step and highest cleared Outskirts stage. If you send it, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to add. We use these to answer you and investigate the issue. Do not include passwords, access tokens, payment details or unrelated private information.

Providers, purposes and security

We use Unity (UGS) for authentication, Cloud Code, Cloud Save, social game data, leaderboards and consent-based Analytics. Google provides Play distribution and handles the studio support mailbox. These providers process information to deliver their services. Other players receive the game information described above. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

We process account and game information to provide the game you request, use our legitimate interests to protect the service and answer support requests, and rely on your consent for optional analytics. Where the law requires records, we process them to meet that obligation.

The app sends game and analytics data using encrypted HTTPS connections. Your chosen email provider handles email transmission under its own security practices. Encryption in transit does not mean end-to-end encryption of alliance chat.

Unity and its providers may process information outside your country. For EU and UK transfers that require safeguards, we rely on the applicable standard contractual clauses and UK transfer terms in Unity's Data Processing Addendum. Contact us for information about these safeguards. See also Unity's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

How long we keep information

  • Account and game data: while your account is active. We delete inactive accounts after 24 months without a login.
  • Alliance chat messages: up to 90 days; a shorter history may appear in the game.
  • Feedback and support emails: up to 12 months after receipt.
  • Analytics: only with consent, under Unity's retention rules. Unity keeps raw events for 13 months; player metrics and profiles remain until you or we request deletion.
  • Deletion requests: we delete your account and associated data within 30 days of your request. The in-game route removes the live account and game data immediately after the two-step confirmation succeeds. Analytics and other associated records complete deletion within the 30-day window.
  • Backups: deleted data rolls off backups within a further 30 days, at most 60 days from the request. We do not use those copies for normal operations and reapply deletions if we restore a backup.

A deletion request overrides the normal chat, support and analytics retention periods for information associated with your account. We keep no account-related records after deletion except (1) records the law requires (there are none today) and (2) a small one-way hashed security code that cannot be turned back into your player ID and contains no game data, chat or progress. We keep it only to stop a deleted account from being restored and to safely finish a deletion that was interrupted. If a legal requirement applies, we will explain which records we must keep and why.

Delete your account

In the game, open Settings → Delete account. Complete both confirmations, including typing DELETE. After successful deletion, the game signs you out and returns to the boot screen. You lose your progress, alliance membership, chat associated with your account, leaderboard entries and world-map city and marches, along with your UGS account and cloud saves. Deletion cannot be undone.

You can also email abjstudiosgames@gmail.com with the subject Last Lantern deletion and your player ID from Settings → Account. Ask to delete your account and associated data. We do not have an email linked to your anonymous sign-in, so we verify control of the account before deleting it; a player ID or sending email address alone is not proof. If you cannot find your ID or access the app, describe the problem so we can help verify the account. Do not send credentials or identity documents unless we explain a necessary verification step.

See the account deletion page for both routes. Uninstalling, clearing app data or signing out does not delete cloud data and may make an unlinked account harder to recover. This website has no deletion form or submission backend.

Your privacy rights

Contact abjstudiosgames@gmail.com to request access to, a copy of, correction of or deletion of your information, or to object to processing. You can withdraw analytics consent in Settings without losing access to the game. Withdrawal does not affect processing that took place before you withdrew consent. We ask only for the information needed to verify and handle your request.

Where EU or UK GDPR applies, you can also ask us to restrict processing or provide a portable copy where applicable, and complain to your local data protection authority or the UK's Information Commissioner's Office. Where California privacy law applies, you can request the categories and specific information we hold, access, correction and deletion, and use an authorized agent subject to verification. We do not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. We do not sell or share information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We respond within the deadlines required by applicable law.

Children, purchases and changes

Last Lantern is for ages 13+ and is not directed at children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes a child under 13 has given us information should contact abjstudiosgames@gmail.com so we can investigate and delete it. Regional rules may require parental consent for players aged 13 or older; contact us about a child's information or consent.

In-app purchases are not yet available. If we introduce them, Google Play will process payments; we will update this policy to explain purchase validation and records before purchases become available.

We will update this policy when our practices change, change the effective date and explain material changes in the game where appropriate.
