Delete your Last Lantern account

ABJ Studios Games · Effective date: 17 October 2026

You can delete your account in the game or request deletion by email. Both routes cover your Last Lantern account and associated data.

Delete in the game

  1. Open Settings → Delete account.
  2. Read the warning and complete both confirmations, including typing DELETE.
  3. After the request succeeds, we remove your live account and game data immediately. The game signs you out and returns to the boot screen.

Deletion cannot be undone. If the request fails or the game is unavailable, use the email route below.

Request deletion by email

  1. Find your player ID in Settings → Account. You do not have an email address linked to your anonymous game sign-in.
  2. Email abjstudiosgames@gmail.com with the subject Last Lantern deletion. Ask us to delete your account and associated data, and include your player ID.
  3. We verify control of the account before deleting it. A player ID or the email address you send from alone does not prove ownership. If you cannot access the app or find your ID, describe the problem so we can help verify your account.
Prepare deletion email

This link opens your email app for you to review and send the message. If it does not open, copy the address above into your email service. This website has no request form or submission backend; visiting the page does not send a request.

Do not send passwords, access tokens, payment details or unrelated identity documents. Clearing app data or reinstalling may make an unlinked account harder to verify.

What we delete

We delete your UGS player account, cloud saves and game progress: city, buildings, resources, citizens, troops, heroes, equipment, formations, tutorial and battle progress, world-map city position, research, marches and rally participation. We remove your alliance membership, chat associated with your account, leaderboard entries, and associated account, migration, tester and support records. We also request deletion of analytics associated with your account.

Other players may have copied messages you sent. Deleting your account cannot remove copies outside our control.

Timing and retained records

We complete deletion of your account and associated data within 30 days of your request. The in-game route removes live account and game data immediately after both confirmations succeed; analytics and other associated records complete deletion within the 30-day window. For email requests, we will contact you if we need information to verify the account.

Deleted data rolls off backups within a further 30 days, at most 60 days from your request. We do not use these copies for normal operations and reapply deletions if we restore a backup.

We keep nothing associated with your account after deletion except records the law requires; there are no such retained records today. If a legal requirement applies, we will explain which records we must keep and why.

Without a deletion request, we keep account/game data while the account is active and delete inactive accounts after 24 months without a login. Alliance chat lasts up to 90 days, feedback emails up to 12 months after receipt, and consent-based analytics follows Unity's retention rules. Your deletion request overrides these normal periods for information associated with your account. See the full retention schedule.

Before deletion

You lose your progress and access to the deleted account. Uninstalling the game, clearing app data or signing out does not delete cloud records. In-app purchases are not yet available.

For access, correction or other privacy requests, email abjstudiosgames@gmail.com. Read the privacy policy for details about information use and your rights.
