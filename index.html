A home within the light

Survival · City building · Strategy

Every lamp is a little hope.

Grow a settlement around the light, care for its people, and prepare your heroes for the darkness beyond.

01 / BUILD

Give the city room to grow

Raise the Great Lantern through Ages and Flames. As the light reaches farther, choose where to build and keep the city supplied with food, timber and oil.

02 / CARE

Keep your people cared for

Balance housing, meals, health and work. Assign workers and watch morale as your settlement finds its feet in the night.

03 / PREPARE

Face the Shadows

Recruit heroes, improve their equipment and arrange your team on lit and dark Outskirts tiles. Find a formation that brings them home.
